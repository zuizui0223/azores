# Activation transferability audit

## Question

Can the strong pooled Durif-stage activation result be upgraded to:

> the stage effect independently replicates in every one of the six projects?

No.

## What is supported

The pooled project × release-year adjusted model gives:

- OR per Durif stage: **2.08**;
- 95% CI: **1.56–2.76**.

Leave-one-project-out estimates remain positive, showing that no single project is required for the pooled direction.

## Why that is not six independent replications

Direct project-specific logistic models were inspected using the same within-project release-year, body-length and release-timing structure.

Several projects are estimable, but two projects have very high initiation fractions in their informative strata and show quasi/complete separation in an unpenalized project-specific logistic fit.

Examples from the direct audit:

- 2013 Albertkanaal: 83 initiators among 94 rows retained in its informative strata;
- 2019 Grotenete: 33 initiators among 38 rows.

The resulting unpenalized stage coefficients are numerically unstable and cannot be interpreted as reliable project-level effect sizes.

Thus:

> **leave-one-project-out robustness demonstrates that the pooled result is not driven by one project; it does not demonstrate six separately estimable replications.**

## Consequence for the manuscript

Allowed:

> Across the pooled multi-project analysis, more advanced Durif stage robustly predicted migration activation, and the positive pooled effect persisted when each project was omitted in turn.

Avoid:

> The activation effect replicated independently in all six projects.

## Relation to post-activation speed

The post-activation speed endpoint is fully estimable in the pooled n=418 dataset and does not show a general Durif gradient.

A separate project-slope test found no supported among-project speed-effect heterogeneity (stage × project p = 0.398).

The combined evidence therefore supports a phase-resolved pooled contrast, but not a universal claim that every individual water body independently exhibits the same phase switch.

## Novelty boundary

Azores is best positioned as:

> **an empirical decomposition showing that the same capture-time readiness axis carries strong pooled information about migration activation/onset but little general information about post-activation speed.**

It should not be promoted to a new universal movement-ecology law or to six independent project-level replications.

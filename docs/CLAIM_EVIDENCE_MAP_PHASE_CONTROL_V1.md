# Claim–evidence map — Azores phase-specific migration paper

| Claim | Evidence | Strength | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Advanced Durif stage predicts migration initiation | Adjusted six-project initiation OR 2.08, CI 1.56–2.76 | Strong developmental | Durif stages already encode migratory readiness; positive control, not novelty alone |
| Advanced stage predicts earlier migration onset | Stratified Cox HR 1.28, CI 1.12–1.45 | Strong developmental | LOPO remains positive but 2015 omission reduces precision |
| Stage is not a general post-initiation speed predictor | Adjusted speed ratio 0.983, CI 0.852–1.134 | Strong null-compatible | Does not prove zero individual-state effect |
| Stage gives weak general completion advantage after initiation | Conditional OR 1.15, CI 0.83–1.59 | Moderate | Project heterogeneity remains |
| Stage effect attenuates after initiation | Direct clustered phase interaction OR ratio 1.81, CI 1.15–2.84, p=0.0099 | **Primary novel evidence** | Developmental, not preregistered |
| External context aligns more with completion than initiation | WRS rho +0.029 vs -0.928 | Bridge evidence | n=6 and project-confounded; not causal |
| Route-specific opportunity matters after activation | Independent Dutch pump/sluice study | Independent corroboration | Published study, not direct reanalysis/replication of phase interaction |
| Migration is sequentially filtered | Combined Gate 1 + Gate 2 evidence | Main ecological synthesis | Do not imply mutually exclusive internal/external control |
| Readiness and escapement are distinct conservation bottlenecks | Biological inference from sequential gates | Applied implication | Needs cautious wording; not management experiment |

## Primary paper claim

> **The predictive effect of internal migratory readiness is phase dependent: strong at migration activation and significantly attenuated during subsequent progression.**

Everything else supports or interprets this claim.

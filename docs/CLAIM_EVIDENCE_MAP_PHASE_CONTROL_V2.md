## Primary paper claim

> **Capture-time migratory readiness strongly predicts entry into and timing of migration, but among eels that cross this stage-dependent activation filter the same capture-stage gradient does not provide a transferable general ranking of post-activation speed across the two progression summaries tested; system-specific progression effects can still occur.**

## Explicitly not claimed

- non-membership in the upstream terminal positive set equals failure;
- escapement probability has been estimated;
- external conditions causally replace internal state;
- internal state becomes biologically irrelevant after activation;
- the post-activation speed contrast is a causal estimate of stage attenuation; conditioning on activation selects a stage-dependent subset.

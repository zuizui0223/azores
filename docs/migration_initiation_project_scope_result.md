# Project-level scope of the Durif–activation association

## Question

The pooled six-project analysis gives an adjusted initiation OR of **2.08 per FIII -> FIV -> FV increment**.

This audit asks:

> **Is that association broadly positive across systems, or generated by one project?**

This is a post-hoc scope audit. It does not replace the pooled project-year model.

## Stably estimable projects

Using the same endpoint, expert corrections, release-year stratification, body-length adjustment and release-timing adjustment, four projects yield stable standalone ordinal-stage estimates:

| Project | n | initiated | adjusted OR/stage | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Warnow | 141 | 107 | **1.53** | 0.84–2.78 |
| Leopold Canal | 80 | 52 | **2.16** | 1.02–4.59 |
| Scheldt | 126 | 86 | **3.50** | 1.84–6.68 |
| ESGL | 46 | 12 | **1.53** | 0.71–3.32 |

All four point estimates are positive.

Cochran heterogeneity across these four coefficients:

- Q = **4.14**
- df = **3**
- p = **0.246**
- DerSimonian-Laird tau² = **0.0467**

There is no strong evidence here for large between-project heterogeneity.

## Two projects are not individually estimable in the same way

Albert Canal and Grote Nete contain sparse stage cells with near-perfect initiation outcomes.

Their standalone ordinal logistic coefficients undergo quasi/separation and should not be interpreted as finite biological effect sizes.

This is a design limitation, not evidence for zero or opposite effects.

## Combined with the speed scope audit

The useful phase contrast is now:

### Activation

- pooled adjusted OR/stage = **2.08**;
- leave-one-project-out direction remains positive;
- four standalone estimable projects all have positive adjusted stage coefficients;
- no strong heterogeneity is detected among those four.

### Post-activation overall speed

- pooled ratio/stage = **0.983**;
- project-specific speed ratios range roughly **0.915–1.272**;
- heterogeneity Q = **2.47**, df = **5**, p = **0.781**.

Thus the data are not well described by a hidden mixture in which some projects have a huge positive speed-stage effect and others a huge negative one.

## Updated interpretation

The strongest safe ecological statement is:

> **Morphological migratory readiness shows a broad association with whether downstream migration becomes behaviourally expressed, whereas the same readiness axis provides little general information about the source-defined overall speed of already activated migration.**

Do not write:

> the same activation effect is identical in every water body.

Do not write:

> internal state ceases to matter after departure.

Different progression endpoints, including reach-level passage rates, can retain stage information.
